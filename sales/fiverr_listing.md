## Short Positioning

## Description

If you are spending hours every week copying data, following up with leads, organizing information, answering repetitive questions, or moving work between apps, you may not need another expensive software subscription. You may simply need the right workflow.

I will audit your current process and identify where automation can realistically save time, reduce missed follow-ups, and make routine work easier to manage.

### What you can receive

Depending on the package:

- a map of your current workflow;
- 5 automation opportunities ranked by value, difficulty, and risk;
- up to 3 detailed workflow blueprints;
- recommended tools, including low-cost alternatives where appropriate;
- human approval and fallback points for safer automation;
- ready-to-use prompts, SOPs, or message templates;
- a practical implementation order.

### Good fit for

- solo founders and consultants;
- agencies and small service businesses;
- small e-commerce teams;
- creators and lean operations teams;
- businesses with repetitive admin, lead, content, or data tasks.

### Important

I do not promise guaranteed revenue or fully hands-off automation. The goal is a practical, maintainable workflow that matches your actual business process and constraints.

Before ordering, please prepare a short description of your workflow, the tools you currently use, and the repetitive tasks you want to improve.

## Buyer Requirements

Please answer:

1. What does your business do?
2. What are the 3 most repetitive tasks in your week?
3. Which task causes the most delays or missed follow-ups?
4. What tools do you currently use?
5. Describe one workflow from start to finish.

Do not send passwords, API keys, payment-card data, identity documents, or unnecessary sensitive information.

## FAQ

### Will this automatically make my business more money?

No one can responsibly guarantee that. The audit focuses on measurable operational improvements such as time saved, faster response, fewer missed follow-ups, and reduced repetitive work.

### Do I need paid AI tools?

Not necessarily. I prioritize your existing tools and low-cost options. If a paid tool or API is genuinely useful, I will identify the cost before recommending implementation.

### Can you build the automation too?

Yes, when the workflow is suitable for a lightweight implementation. More complex integrations may require a custom quote.

### Can you work with my existing CRM / email / spreadsheet setup?

Usually yes. The audit starts from your existing stack rather than forcing a new one.

### Do you need my passwords?

No. Passwords should never be sent in the order chat. Implementation should use appropriate account invitations, limited permissions, or client-side setup whenever possible.
